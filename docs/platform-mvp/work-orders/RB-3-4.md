## Goal

An administrator opens «Rollen» under Verwaltung and sees each role as the mock-up draws it: what it may do, what it
may read, who is in it (RB-3). She creates, renames and deletes roles, changes their functions and adds and removes
people (RB-4). A new role gets its own group in the Core before it is saved; Administration and Mitglied get theirs
when she says so. The people page offers roles instead of the ten keys. The design's rules 1, 3, 4 and 5 hold on every
path, and every change stands in the Platform's audit log by id.

## What RB-3 and RB-4 do not do

- **Data** (RB-5): sources, folders and documents, «+ Daten hinzufügen», the dry run, the four-eyes path for data,
  adopting an existing Core group as a role's group (decision 5). The part «Darf lesen» is read only here.
- **Agents** (RB-6, RB-7; RB-8 dropped, founder 2026-11-21: "Core gateway only"): the Knovas Assistent and external agents as members. The
  mock-up's two agent rows are not drawn.
- **The probe view** (RB-9) and the switch «Aus / Probe / Ein».
- **Splitting `admin`** (RB-10).

The frozen file pins nothing that these items take away again: it asks for the words «wirkt mit «Ein»», not for an
empty data part, and it never asks that an agent row or a data form be absent.

## What to build (the frozen tests pin it)

### 1. The routes

All in the console's blueprint (`admin`), each behind the console's own `require_admin` (its view carries
`allowed_roles == frozenset({"admin"})`; signed out 302 to the sign-in, every other function and an account without

| Method and path | Form fields | What it does | Answers |

| `GET /admin/roles/<id>` | | the page, on that role | 200; unknown, foreign or malformed id 404 |
| `POST /admin/roles/create` | `name`, `description` (optional), `function` (repeated, optional) | «Rolle anlegen» (§ 4) | success: the new role's page (a redirect to it, or drawn at once); refused 400; Core refused 502 |

### 3. Reading

The page reads the store (`list_roles`, `get_role`) and the people (`gate.users().list_all()`). It reads nothing from
the Core and writes nothing anywhere: no GET of these pages makes a Core call that writes, and the app's start makes
none (RB-1/RB-2 rule 9).

### 4. The actions, in their order

Every POST: the gate, the token, the role (404), then **everything the store would refuse, before the Core is asked**
(a name of 1 to 80 characters after trimming and collapsing white space, unique regardless of case; functions among
the ten; `admin` only in Administration), then the Core, then the store and the audit row in one transaction
(`conn.transaction()`).

- **Rolle anlegen.** `client.create_access_group(<the role's name>, parent=None)` first: a root group. Only when it
  answers with a `group_id`: `create_role(name=…, description=…, functions=…, core_group_id=<that id>, by=<me>)`.
  `None` (the client's 404) or an exception: 502, no role. If saving fails after the Core created the group, delete
  that group again (best effort, logged by id) and say so.
- **Umbenennen.** Roles of the tenant's own only (decision 3). With a Core group:
  `client.rename_access_group(<group id>, <new name>)` first; the group id stays. `None` or an exception: 502, the old
  name stays. Without a Core group (a role the move of RB-1 created): the store alone.
- **Darf tun.** `set_functions`. No Core call. Rule 1 below.
- **Mitglieder.** `add_member` / `remove_member` with `by=<me>`. No Core call: members reach the Core in the next
  claim (design § 3). The store's `LastAdministratorError` and `TooManyGroupsError` become 400 with their message.
- **Löschen** (design § 3: data, members, Core group, role; data is RB-5's). Administration and Mitglied: 400, no
  Core call. Otherwise, in this order:
  1. remove every member (`remove_member`, committed, each audited);
  2. with a Core group: `client.delete_access_group(<group id>)`.
     - `True`: the group is deleted; go on.
     - `False` (the client's 404): **the group is already gone** (removed in the Core directly); go on, and the
       audit row of step 3 carries `"core_group_already_absent": true` (decision 11).
     - An exception (409: the Core refuses to delete a group that data still name; or any other error) is a
       refusal: the role stays, with its group and functions and without its members; answer 502 and say that
       Knovas kept the group and the role is kept without members (decision 6);
  3. `delete_role`.
  Without a Core group: steps 1 and 3, no Core call.
- **Mit Knovas verbinden** (decision 2). For Administration without a group: the tenant's administrator group, read
  with `client.access_groups()` (`is_admin` true; `admin_doc_fields.admin_group_ids` reads it); exactly one, or
  nothing is linked and the answer is 502. Never create a group for Administration. For Mitglied without a group:
  `client.create_access_group("Mitglied", parent=None)`, then `set_core_group`. A role that already has a group is
  left alone, so a second call sends nothing to the Core.

administrator. Pinned:

| `role.created` | `role`, the new role | its Core group id, its functions |
| `role.renamed` | `role`, the role | (ids only; no name, neither old nor new) |

## Rules

1. **No role grants more than its creator holds** (design rule 1). Functions: the acting person may give a function
   only if she holds it, and `admin` counts as holding every function (it passes the console's, the Builder's and the
   Experimente module's gates, design § 3). Only administrators reach these routes, so in RB-4 this refuses nobody;
   write it as one function the routes call (for example `grantable(actor)`), so RB-10 can narrow `admin` in one
   place (decision 4). `admin` belongs to Administration alone (RB-1/RB-2 rule 2). Data: RB-5. **Granting oneself a
   role needs no second person** (founder): no role change queues an approval, with the administrators' bypass on or
   off.
2. **The last administrator keeps the role** (rule 3). No path leaves Administration without an active person: the
   remove form, the people page, Administration's functions, deleting Administration. Deactivating the last
   administrator cannot happen through the people page (the actor is an active member of Administration and cannot
   deactivate herself, `admin.py` today); keep that check.
   *Coordinator's amendment (2026-11-21, from RB-1/RB-2's verification, F3):* a race is still open: removing B from
   Administration while B deactivates A can leave no active administrator. Deactivating, deleting a person and
   every removal from Administration take the same lock (the one RB-2 takes for Administration) and re-check
   "at least one other active administrator remains" inside it. Add a two-connection test of your own.
3. **Every change is audited** (rule 4, § 6 above), in the same transaction: an audit row that cannot be written
   undoes the change, and the page does not report success. `audit.record` is best effort and swallows errors, so
   use a write that raises (for example a new function next to it in `identity/audit.py`; `record` stays as it is).
   `actor_email_snapshot` is the audit table's own column and stays as `audit.record` fills it (decision 8).
4. **A change reaches the Core before it counts** (rule 5): a new role's group first, a rename's group first; members
   count from the next claim. Data changes and their four-eyes path `acl_change` are RB-5's.
5. **Nothing at start, nothing on a GET** (RB-1/RB-2 rule 9): the Core is written only on an administrator's POST.
6. **No gate changes.** No route's gate or decorator, no `allowed_roles` other than the console's admin gate, no
   change to `builder/team.py`, `experiments/permissions.py`, `identity/assertion.py`. Keep `ASSIGNABLE_ROLES` in
   `admin.py`: three test files read it; it no longer feeds the people page.
7. **No customer text in logs** (FU-14): ids and counts only, DEBUG included. No role name, description, person name
   or address in a log line or an exception message you raise; the page writes its own words. The group's name sent
   to the Core is the role's name (the Core stores it); it is never logged.
8. **Escaping and addresses:** every name and description escaped (no `|safe`); every href and form action from
   `url_for` (FU-34).
9. **The look:** the parts of PMVP-12 and the Admin look of PMVP-40, brand tokens only, one main action, nothing
   scrolls sideways at 390 px, text contrast 4.5 to 1, a focus ring on every control. If a gate is missed, design § 7
   allows the current Admin look; the structure of § 2 stays.

## Decisions this order takes (draft: for the coordinator to confirm before the tests are frozen)

1. **Class sensitive**, not critical (see the header). It writes access groups to the tenant, but through existing
   client calls on an administrator's action, and changes no table.
2. **Linking Administration and Mitglied is an explicit action** («Mit Knovas verbinden», `POST
   /admin/roles/connect`), not a side effect of the first other action. RB-1/RB-2 asks for "an administrator's
   action"; a hidden link inside "add a member" would make every first action depend on the Core and fail for a
   reason the administrator did not ask about.
3. **Administration and Mitglied keep their names.** The page offers no rename for them and the route refuses it.
   Administration's group is the tenant's administrator group, which this page should not rename; both names are
   the words the people page and the founder use.
4. **Rule 1 for functions: `admin` holds every function.** The strict reading (only functions she literally holds)
   would make a «Freigaben bestätigen» role impossible on a new installation, where nobody holds `approver`. Until
   RB-10 splits `admin`, the check refuses nobody.
5. **Adopting an existing Core group as a role's group moves to RB-5** (RB-1/RB-2 rule 9 had given it to RB-4).
   Adopting a group brings its folder rules and document rights, that is data, and wants RB-5's dry run.
6. **After a Core refusal on delete the members stay removed** (the design's order) and the page says so; the
   administrator may delete again once the data are gone.
7. **The description is set when the role is created.** `RoleStore` has no call to change it; a later item can add
   one.
8. **"Ids only" means the row's target and detail.** `actor_email_snapshot` stays, as for every console action
   today; changing it is `identity/audit.py`'s business, not this item's.
9. **No four-eyes for role changes** (founder: granting oneself a role needs no second person). Data changes keep
   `acl_change` (RB-5).
10. **The audit actions and detail keys are pinned** (§ 6), because the Protokoll page will read them.
11. **A 404 from the Core on delete means the group is already gone** (the coordinator, 2026-11-21, replacing the
    draft's "a 404 is a refusal"): the role is deleted and its `role.deleted` row says
    `core_group_already_absent: true`. Otherwise a role whose group was removed in the Core could never be deleted.
    A 409 or any other refusal keeps the role (decision 6).
12. **The people page writes one `user.roles_changed` row per save**, not one row per role.
