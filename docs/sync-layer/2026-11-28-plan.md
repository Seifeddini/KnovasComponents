## 5. Risks to the dates

- **The founder's time is the critical path.** Every acceptance and every server01 run is his. A missed day moves the items after it by that day.
- **SS-960's cause may be in the graph engine, not the runner.** The retry makes the apply whole either way. Removing the cause may need its own issue.
- **The Knovas GmbH Brain folder's uncommitted work** can block SS-959's load. That is why D9 keeps the files in KnovasComponents and lets the folder take them.
- **N4 (SS-580)** moves two backends and a component with 879 tests. 22 Dec is the most ambitious date that is possible; early January is the honest fallback.
- **Deployment state is not visible from git:** which `BRAIN_WRITER` runs, and which switches are on at server01. On Mon 30 Nov the founder confirms them in one look, or SS-961's first item reads them out.
